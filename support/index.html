Boombaland Support

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Need help with Boombaland? Email info.tomdebruijn@gmail.com. Please tell us your device (for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8), the game version (Settings › About) and what happened. We answer as soon as we can, and within one month.

Questions

I bought something and did not get it

First try Settings › Account › Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. If it is still missing, email us with the receipt from Apple or Google.

How do I get my No Ads or Unlimited Energy back on a new device?

Install Boombaland, sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, and tap Settings › Account › Restore Purchases. Permanent purchases come back. Coins, gems and energy you bought are used up when delivered and cannot be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, because we never receive your payment details:

  • Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

How do I change my ad or tracking choice?

In Europe and the US states with privacy laws, use Settings › About › Privacy choices. On iPhone and iPad, use iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug

Thank you! Email info.tomdebruijn@gmail.com with what you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your Boombaland data in the game, or ask us by email if you cannot open the game any more.

In the game

Open Settings › Account › Delete my data and confirm twice. While you have more than one save, this deletes the save you are playing; delete each save to delete everything. When no save is left, it deletes:

  • on your device: your progress, settings, wallet (coins, gems, energy and the transaction numbers of your purchases), achievements, player name and date of birth;
  • your cloud copy in your iCloud (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games Saved Games (Android);
  • at Unity: the Unity Cloud Save copy of your save and your Unity player account (the player ID and its link to the Unity account or Google Play Games account you signed in with).

The device needs an internet connection for the cloud copies. If the Unity copy cannot be deleted at that moment, it stays with Unity until you sign in again and delete it, or until you ask us by email.

By email

Email info.tomdebruijn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us which devices you played on and, if you used Cloud save across devices, the Unity account or Google Play Games account you signed in with, so we can find your Unity player. We may ask you to confirm that the request is yours. Within 30 days we delete your Unity player account and its Unity Cloud Save copy, and we tell you what we deleted; if we cannot find your Unity player from the details you gave, we tell you that within the same 30 days. We cannot reach the files on your device or the copy in your own iCloud or Google Play Games account: uninstall the game to remove the files, and delete the cloud copy in your Apple or Google account settings.

What is kept, and why

  • Purchase records held by Apple and Google. The stores keep their own record of what you bought, under their policies and tax law; we cannot delete them. That record is also what lets Restore Purchases give back permanent items such as No Ads. Coins and gems you bought are lost when your data is deleted.
  • Your Unity account itself (the login with your email address, made at Unity) is Unity's, not ours; ask Unity to delete it, under Unity's privacy policy.
  • Google's advertising data is kept under Google's policy. You can manage it at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • Your email to us is kept as long as we need it to handle your request, and at most 2 years.

See section 7.1 of the Privacy Policy for the same details.
