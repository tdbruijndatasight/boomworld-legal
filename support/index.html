Boombaland Support

Need help with Boombaland? Email info.tomdebruijn@gmail.com. Please tell us your device (for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8), the game version (Settings › About) and what happened. We answer as soon as we can, and within one month.

Questions

I bought something and did not get it

First try Settings › Account › Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. If it is still missing, email us with the receipt from Apple or Google.

How do I get my No Ads or Unlimited Energy back on a new device?

Install Boombaland, sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, and tap Settings › Account › Restore Purchases. Permanent purchases come back. Coins, gems and energy you bought are used up when delivered and cannot be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, because we never receive your payment details:

  • Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

How do I delete my data?

In the game, go to Settings › Account › Delete my data. It deletes your progress, settings, coins and gems from your device and starts the game over. Uninstalling Boombaland also deletes everything. We keep no player data on any server. Google's advertising data is controlled at https://myadcenter.google.com.

How do I change my ad or tracking choice?

In Europe, use Settings › About › Manage consent. On iPhone and iPad, use iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug
